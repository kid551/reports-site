Afternoon Session - 2025 Meeting

18 章节0 条发言时长 2:03:31发布 2025-05-05CNBC 原页 ↗

As the afternoon session is ending, Warren Buffett surprises everyone with his intention to step down as Berkshire's CEO at the end of the year. Before that he and Vice Chairman Greg Abel cover the U.S. government's "unsustainable" deficit spending, why the health care "tapeworm" won, and Abel's plans to be more active than Buffett in managing the company's subsidiaries.
